# Product Positioning

## Product Category

The Award Nomination App is an AI-enabled employee recognition governance platform.

It sits between:

- Employee recognition tools.
- HR workflow automation.
- Fraud and integrity analytics.
- Payroll coordination.
- Executive HR analytics.

## Positioning Statement

For organizations that need trusted employee recognition programs, the Award Nomination App provides a tenant-configurable award nomination, approval, integrity monitoring, analytics, and payroll coordination platform. Unlike simple recognition portals or generic workflow tools, it combines workflow automation, fraud-aware review, graph analytics, auditability, multilingual tenant configuration, and Azure-native operations in one SaaS-ready system.

## Target Customers

Best-fit customers include:

- Mid-market and enterprise organizations with monetary recognition programs.
- HR teams managing award budgets across departments or geographies.
- Organizations concerned about fairness, favoritism, or inconsistent award practices.
- Companies using Microsoft Entra ID and Azure.
- Organizations that need payroll-connected bonus awards.
- Grant, nonprofit, public sector, or regulated organizations requiring auditability.

| Persona | Buying motivation |
| --- | --- |

| Compliance or risk leader | Reduce abuse risk and improve traceability. |

## Core Pain Points

- Recognition programs are manual, inconsistent, or opaque.
- Monetary awards can be overused, duplicated, or concentrated among select groups.
- Approvals lack analytics and auditability.
- HR teams cannot easily identify reciprocal nomination patterns or nomination rings.
- Payroll execution is disconnected from approval.
- Executives lack live visibility into spend, risk, and forecasted review load.
- Multilingual or region-specific configuration is difficult in generic tools.

## Differentiators

| Differentiator | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Tenant-configurable SaaS model | Supports multiple customer organizations without separate codebases. |
| Fraud-aware workflow | Integrity screening happens before manager approval, not after damage is done. |
| HRBP review queue | Keeps humans in control of high-risk decisions. |
| Graph pattern detection | Finds collusion and structural anomalies that row-level rules miss. |
| Semantic description checks | Detects duplicate, low-fit, transactional, or low-quality nomination text. |
| Payroll broker | Connects award approval to payout operations. |
| AI analytics workspace | Lets admins ask questions and investigate patterns from system data. |
| Azure-native deployment | Fits enterprise Microsoft environments and security expectations. |
| Observability built in | Supports real operational support and enterprise due diligence. |

## Relationship to Integrity Sentinel

- HR recognition.
- Payroll adjustments.
- Procurement approvals.
- Expense reimbursements.
- Grant awards.
- Access requests.
- Vendor onboarding.

The Award Nomination App provides a practical proof point for the larger platform story: workflow + risk analytics + human review + audit + AI investigation.
